LIFE-SAVING HABBIT

The Life-Saving Habit: Hair donation is a truly remarkable experience, everyone must experience it atleast once in life.

Choosing to donate hair is a deeply personal way to offer support and strength to someone navigating the challenges of cancer treatment. This simple act of cutting away something of your own provides the foundation for a high-quality wig, helping a patient regain a sense of normalcy and confidence during a difficult time. It is a powerful reminder that while hair is temporary, the impact of such a selfless gift can be lasting, offering a tangible sign of care and solidarity to a stranger in need.

Standards for Hair Donation:

Most organizations require a minimum of 8 to 12 inches.

Hair must be completely dry before it is mailed to prevent mold. It should also be free of any styling products like hairspray or gel.
Use rubber bands to secure the hair in a ponytail or braid at both ends before cutting. This keeps the strands aligned, which is vital for wig-making.
Double-check your chosen organization's rules regarding color-treated or grey hair, as some accept it while others require "virgin" (unprocessed) hair.
